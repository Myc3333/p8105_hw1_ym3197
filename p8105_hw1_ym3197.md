The penguins data frame captures biological measurements from a sample
of penguins. It records categorical information such as species, island,
and sex, alongside continuous physical traits including bill length,
bill depth, flipper length, and body mass. The dataset spans r
nrow(penguins) observations and r ncol(penguins) variables. A key
summary statistic is the mean flipper length, which evaluates to r
mean(penguins\$flipper_length_mm, na.rm = TRUE) %\>% round(1) mm. Now we
create a scatter

``` r
# Create the scatterplot
ggplot(data = penguins, aes(x = bill_length_mm, y = flipper_length_mm, color = species)) +
  geom_point() +
  theme_minimal()
```
